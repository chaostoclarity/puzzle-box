# Seal IX: the last stair and the cabinet (rooms 17 and 18). Endings and Mercy.

## Room 17, the last stair (room option "gravity")
- Down is wherever you look: every turn rolls the slab's marble toward the wall you now face. Dust on the floor shows the last direction.
- The maze on the slab (6x6): from north, E S E S W S E S E S, a quarter turn at a time. The wrong stair on the south wall resets the marble. Sinking it unbars the ceiling door.
- Dream sight hides the dreamed slab on the west wall: behind it, the Elder Sign and fragment IX.
- Elder Sign on the ceiling door; go through into the cabinet.

## Room 18, inside the cabinet (interface puzzles)
- Lens socket ("lens_socket"): face north and raise the Lens; the cabinet takes it. The Lens button goes dark for good.
- Tumblers: eight, one per tray slot, up when that slot is full. The engraving wants slots 1-3 and 8 up. Slot 8 is the one given to the keepers in seal VI. Pick up the five brass teeth, drop two in the box, turn the key.
- The Whisper's mouth asks for the hint button. Give it: the Hint button disappears (flag hints_burned).
- The ninth door. Endings by condition:
  - THE NINTH SEAL: 8 or more of Wren's 9 fragments.
  - THE VESSEL: 3 or more bargains, Mercy off.
  - THE KEEPER: otherwise.

## Mercy (title screen)
A setting kept across games. With Mercy on, the Whisper's help costs no page and counts no bargain.
