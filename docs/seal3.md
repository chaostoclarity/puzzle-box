# Seal III: puzzle chains (Act II, Doubt)

The sanitarium. Act II hides a second truth under the first and moves things when you look away. Ada Merrow, admitted 1911, was a seeker before Wren.

## Room 5: Ada Merrow's cell (teaches working items in the tray; curse: the room changes when you turn)
1. Diary sewn into the mattress: the stool walks when you don't watch; on the 4th look (and every 10th after) it's under the window; her key is in two halves; her pins are on her handkerchief, the torn row "where they never look".
2. A loose spring on the cot: bedspring wire. In the tray, bend it into a hook.
3. Look down: the hook fishes half a key from the drain.
4. Tally marks on the south wall count your turns. The stool moves every turn (turns mod 5: N, W, hidden, E, S). Face the window on the 4th, 14th or 24th turn: climb and take the other half from the sill.
5. In the tray, put the halves together: Ada's key.
6. Lift the sewing box (made of cot slats), unlock it. Its lid is a 3x3 pincushion: each pin flips itself and its neighbours (lights out).
   The target: the handkerchief's two columns plus the torn third column, which is on the ceiling, only through the Lens.
7. Inside the lid, Ada sketched her key: a zigzag. Bend the wire again in the tray.
8. Dive into the needle case; the zigzag wire opens it. A locket.
9. In the tray, pull the locket apart: it folds into an eye tile (and the photograph inside is of Thorne, dated 1911).
10. The tile goes into the socket in the cell door. Out.

## Room 6: the records room (teaches cross-referencing; curse: the file about you)
1. Admissions ledger (desk): Ada, No. 0417, 1911. The last line, in your own hand: Thorne, Elias, No. 0918, 1928.
2. Filing rules (west wall, plus the row chart beside the drawers): ward by number, row by the year's last digit.
3. Index under the drawer wall: ward wheel, row wheel, lever. B-1: Ada's file ("discharged 1912") and a punched card. Through the Lens, her drawer is stamped DIED IN CARE 1913.
4. Lay the card over the Rules of the Ward (south wall): E X T 2 0 6.
5. Dial 206 on the telephone (west wall, silent). A capsule drops into the pneumatic tube in the ceiling: the effects key.
6. Lift the EFFECTS box on the desk, unlock it: a bone box.
7. D-4 is your file, dated next year. Its first line describes you opening it. It says the bone box opens to the year Ada really died.
8. Dive into the bone box: three numbered rings, IN MEMORIAM 1..., set 9 1 3 and press the pin. The third seal.

## Hub 103: Thorne's study, third visit. Three sockets lit, Wren's limit. The drawer gives a deed to the Gardner farm (seal IV, not built yet).
