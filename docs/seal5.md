# Seal V: the ice (rooms 9 and 10, hub 105)

Act III begins. Theme: the seal asks for your words. You keep warm by burning your own journal.

## Room 9, Hut 4 (Kingsport Antarctic Survey)
- On entering, a blank page is added to the journal. The stove asks (dialog) for the blank page first: fair warning that pages will burn.
- Heat (tick puzzle): each page burned raises heat by 1 (max 3); heat falls by 1 every 6 moves. Thermometer and stove art follow heat.
- Heat 1: window frost thins (stakes visible, numbers unreadable). Heat 2: stake numbers readable (red 3, blue 8, green 1); the specimen case thaws off the bench.
- Survey log: the case is locked to the stakes "red, then blue, then green". Dials commit on the latch: [3, 8, 1].
- Frozen tin in the case: use on a lit stove to thaw, then pry it open in the tray: bone flute plus the score (journal: 3, 1, 5, 2, torn).
- Heat 3 frees the floor hatch. Down to room 10.

## Room 10, the shaft
- Seven ice chimes (melody). The score gives the first four; after those, the ice echoes the next note (the chime shivers). Order 3 1 5 2 7 4 6.
- The chimes open a glass chamber: three tone holes, each hums one of the echoed notes. Silent sequence in the order the ice hummed them (right, left, middle). Inside: the torn end of the score, Wren's fragment V.
- Frozen man (Halvorsen). Lens 2 reads the note in his pocket: the star in the plug turns one point per page the fire ate. His count (4) is his, not yours.
- Star plug: rotate the star (5 points) to pages-burned mod 5, commit by blowing the bone flute into the hole. Seal stone V inside.
- Ladder up to hub 105.

## Hub 105
Fifth socket. The drawer gives a key to the library map room (seal VI, the Nameless City). Routes to 99 until seal VI ships.
