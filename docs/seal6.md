# Seal VI: the city under the sand (rooms 11 and 12, hub 106)

The keepers count in dots and bars: a dot is one, a bar is three. The weights in room 11 teach it; room 12 depends on it.

## Room 11, the antechamber
- Tile panel (cycler + commit): four turning tiles must make the glyph over the door. The glyph is worn away; Lens 2 shows it whole. Bronze stud commits. Reward: weight 1.
- Offering (ask): the stone mouth asks for a tray slot. Giving it sets slots_given; the tray shows an eye in that slot for the rest of the game (seal IX uses tray slots as tumblers). Reward: weight 9.
- Hourglass (tick with "while"): turning it buries nothing new but strands weight 3 in the top bulb with the sand. The sand only falls on moves made while you are not facing the east wall. Five unobserved moves empty it.
- Scales (new: flag comparisons "wl==wr", "wl<wr"): the idol weighs 7. Weights go on either pan; the pointer shows the heavier side. 7 + 3 = 9 + 1. Press the red stone on the altar to commit. Opens the low door.

## Room 12, the hall of keepers
- Mural (new kind "swap"): five panels out of order; tap two to swap. Order by the sand rising. Press the round stone to commit. Reveals a seven-fingered hand with five fingers numbered in dots and bars.
- Coffin hand lock (silent sequence): press fingers 5, 2, 7, 1, 4.
- Keeper inside; Lens 2 shows "E.T. HERE" on its wrappings: fragment VI.
- Reliquary (radial, 8 positions): the coffin plate reads 3 and 7 in dots and bars, small circle under the first, large under the second. Inner ring 3, outer 7, push the pin. Seal stone VI.
- The stair gate opens once the stone is taken. Up to hub 106 (routes to 99 until seal VII ships).
