# Seal IV: the Gardner farm (Act II, Doubt)

The farm west of Arkham where a stone fell in the well (The Colour Out of Space, public domain). Theme: growth and decay.

## Room 7: the kitchen (teaches rot: solved parts decay after 8 moves; growth)
1. Nahum's notes: everything cut grows back in eight moves, watch the flower; the pie safe's three latches must be open at once; the bean pod in three breaths; the almanac only reads through the Lens.
2. Look up: the shears are caught in the ceiling roots.
3. The almanac (south wall) through the Lens: March peas, April wheat, May corn, June squash.
4. Pie safe: top latch (cut the root), middle latch (turn tiles so the grey root grows through the tin panel to the latch: a beam puzzle drawn as roots), bottom latch (seed jars for MAR, MAY, JUN: peas, corn, squash, then press its latch).
   Each latch rots shut 8 moves after it opens; the cut flower wilts on the same count. Solve order matters: set the jars, finish the tiles, press the jar latch, cut the root.
5. In the safe: seeds. Plant in the windowsill pot; it grows one stage per move. Pick on the third: a pod shaped like a key. Too late and it rots (you get the beans back).
6. The pod opens the bread tin; inside, Abby's velvet box opens from a button on its bottom: the windlass handle and the back door key.
7. The back door: unlock it, cut the roots (they regrow in 4 moves), go.

## Room 8: the well house (teaches the doubled Lens: see through solid things; the phantom dial)
1. A knothole in the south wall holds Wren's ground lens; combine it with the Lens in the tray: the doubled Lens.
2. Look up: fit the handle to the windlass drum. Each turn changes the chain depth (5 down to 0 up, then down again).
3. Look down the well through the doubled Lens: carvings behind the stones at three depths: I 7, II 2, III 4 (digits that read the same if you're facing the other way).
4. Raise the bucket, lift it: two dials and the shadow of a third. The third exists only through the Lens and still has to be turned. Press the pin.
5. Inside, a stone jar choked with roots: cut them with Nahum's shears. The fourth seal.
Optional, Wren's fragment IV: through the doubled Lens, a note inside the hollow post on the north wall.
