# Seal I: puzzle chains

## Room 1, Wren's study (Act I: honest)
1. Letter on the desk: "our hour, three bells".
2. Wall clock: turn the hand to 3, the base opens: brass key.
3. Walnut box: back plaque shows moon, eye, star. Set the left-face dials: a panel slides: iron key.
4. Iron key on the desk drawer: matches and a photograph (books numbered 1 green, 2 blue, 3 red).
5. Matches on the lamp: the dark east wall lights up.
6. Bookshelf: pull green, blue, red: compartment: Wren's signet ring.
7. Brass key on the walnut box lock: the lid lifts, the box tips to show a smaller box sealed with wax.
8. Touch the small box to dive in. Ring on the wax: it opens: door key.
9. Door key on the study door: the stacks.
Flavor: portrait, cabinet (nine seals, three cracked), window, diploma, sampler, coat, globe.

## Room 2, the restricted stacks (introduces the Lens)
1. Velvet case on the librarian's desk: Wren's Lens.
2. Lens shows I, II, III on three spines in the left stack. Pull slate, ochre, plum: niche: padlock key.
3. Padlock key on the chain: the book-box can be lifted.
4. Lens shows three target marks around the cover's rings. Turn outer to right, middle to down, core to left: the cover opens. The pages are hollowed out around a small reliquary.
5. Dive into the reliquary. Its plate is blank. Through the Lens it reads ELIAS THORNE, and a button appears below it.
6. Press it: the first seal stone. End of Seal I.
Optional: Wren's fragment I, in a book marked W (Lens only) in the right stack.


## Rework (Nov 14, after Eric's playtest: "once you have the Lens the rest is trivial"; "clock puzzles: just rotate until they click")
Room 1: Wren's letter says your hour is under his feet. Look down: a loose board under the desk hides his watch (20 past 10, OUR HOUR).
The clock has an hour and a minute hand (touch near the middle or further out) and a winding key; it only opens when wound at 10:20.
The walnut box's back plaque is a clock-face ring with four symbols; the sampler says READ MY RING FROM OUR HOUR AS THE HANDS GO: star, moon, eye, sun, then press the catch.
Five books on the shelf; the photo shows green out flat, blue half drawn, hand on red. Silent sequence.
Room 2: the Lens shows four symbols on the left stack; Wren's alphabet card on the catalog decodes them: WREN, on the librarian's drawer wheels (8 letters each), then pull.
The book cover's three rings each carry part of one engraved eye; complete the picture, then press the clasp.
