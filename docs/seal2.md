# Seal II: puzzle chains

Act I, Reason. The puzzles still obey physical logic. Seal I was too thin (Eric's playtest, Nov 14), so seal II sets the new bar:
3 nested layers per hero box, 12 to 16 steps per room, clues that must be combined, and the room itself used as a box (ceiling, floor, room state).

## Hub: Thorne's study (rooms 101 and 102)
Between seals. Fit the stone into Wren's cabinet; a drawer opens with the next destination. The study decays each visit:
visit 2 adds piled mail, a covered mirror, salt by the door and stains on the ceiling.

## Room 3: the lamp room, Kingsport light (teaches beams and mirrors, and looking up and down)
1. Keeper's log (east desk): where the locker key is, how the locker bars work, how the tin opens, and that he tied the lamp's cover to the roof.
2. The sailcloth over the lamp is lashed to a cleat on the ceiling. Look up and free it.
3. Barometer (east wall): set it to FAIR. Its little door opens: locker key.
4. Oil locker (south): padlock off with the locker key.
5. Three interlocked bars, each pinned by the one below: bottom to middle, middle to left, top to right, middle to right, bottom to left. The door opens: crank and oil can.
6. Storm lantern from its hook by the west window.
7. Oil can on the lamp, then the lantern on the lamp: the great lamp lights.
8. Lift the brass carriage under the lamp. Fit the crank in its front socket: the mirrors on its top unlock.
9. Beam puzzle on the carriage top (6x6, four mirrors, one post). Out to sea: something on the reef turns to look back (seen in the west window). The lid opens and the crank drops free.
10. Dive into the star case inside. Through the Lens, lines join the stars from a ringed one: touch them in that order.
11. Dive into the keeper's tin inside the star case. Wheels marked FL, S, M; the Notice to Mariners (south wall) says FL 3, every 7 S, seen 9 M: 3 7 9. Hatch key.
12. Look down: the stair hatch in the floor. Keeper's key, then go down.
Optional, Wren's fragment II: send the beam inland first (last mirror to /). It lights a stone roundel on the east wall; read it through the Lens.

## Room 4: the wreck of the Constance (combines tide, items carried from the light, the maze, beams and the Lens; first curse)
The tide is room state: 0 high (water to 9), 1 middle (start, to 5), 2 low (to 1.5), 3 past the third mark (the sea is gone). Water blocks what's under it.
1. Captain's log (east chart table): the pump and sea-cock hold her at any mark; the key is on the high shelf "where only the flood can carry a man"; roll the pearl home to see the lock; never past the third mark; the glass turns what brass cannot.
2. Lamp crank from the light fits the bilge pump (south). Oil can from the light frees the rusted sea-cock (west).
3. Open the sea-cock to high water: you float up to the west shelf: crab key.
4. Pump down to the third mark: the sea chest (north) is clear.
5. Lift the chest. Tilt maze on its front: roll the pearl into the hole (s e n e n e s w n e). The keyhole cover drops.
6. Crab key: the lid opens. The false bottom is carved NEVER DRAIN HER PAST THE THIRD MARK.
7. Pump past the third mark. The water leaves, the portholes show a dry seabed and the thing from the reef, closer. A carving on the floor appears. A second keyhole appears in the false bottom.
8. Crab key in the second keyhole: the false bottom lifts. A binnacle.
9. Dive into the binnacle. The storm lantern goes in its socket. Two mirrors can't reach the brass fish's eye; with the Lens on, a mirror appears that brass can't make, and the beam bends to the eye.
10. The second seal is inside. Taking it brings the sea back. Look up: swim out through the skylight.
